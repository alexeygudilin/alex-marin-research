Independent Research

This repository serves as the central access point for independent research conducted and published under the author name Alex Marin, the pen name of Alexey Gudilin.

The research explores mathematical structures, intelligence, cognitive processes, complex systems, adaptation, and structural analysis.

Its central objective is to investigate not only how systems behave, but also the conditions, constraints, and relationships that make particular processes, states, and transformations possible or impossible.

Research Areas

* Mathematics and Structural Modelling: Mathematical models, constraints, invariants, and state spaces.
* Intelligence and Cognitive Systems: Structural foundations of intelligent and cognitive processes.
* Complex Systems and Adaptation: Conditions of stability, transformation, and systemic adaptation.
* Theoretical and Applied Research: Hypothesis testing, model analysis, and exploration of potential applications.

Research Publications

Research materials will be added progressively as they are prepared and reviewed.

The repository will provide research descriptions, links to dedicated research projects, mathematical models, experimental materials, and supporting documentation.

Each published work will include its own description and, where appropriate, version information and development status.

The absence of a particular study from this catalogue does not imply that the research does not exist. The catalogue represents only materials prepared for public presentation.

Research Principles

The research emphasizes structural clarity, verifiable reasoning, reproducibility, and a clear distinction between hypotheses, theoretical models, and validated findings.
